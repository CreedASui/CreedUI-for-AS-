Creed UI - your music

Put your own songs in this folder, named 1.mp3, 2.mp3, 3.mp3 and so on.
Then type /creed music <number> in the game to say how many there are (3 to begin with).

Play them with the round play button on the minimap's edge:
  click        play / pause
  right-click  next song
The volume is in Creed UI Options (/creed), beside the language.

Creed UI comes with no songs. When you update Creed UI, keep a copy of your songs:
installing a new version may replace this folder.
